currency. We had 20% growth in manufacturing, 13% in life sciences. Our European business grew by 10%.

work. This is especially so in financial services, in mortgages, asset management, investment banking,
payments, and in telecom. We also see some impact in hi-tech industry and in parts of retail.

revenue from some of these and other large deals towards the later part of our financial year. Keeping that in
mind, we are changing our revenue growth guidance for this financial year to growth of between 1% to 3.5%
in constant currency.

areas: pyramid efficiency, automation, improvements in critical portfolios, reducing indirect costs, and
communicating and deriving value across our portfolio.

for this financial year remains unchanged at between 20% and 22%.
